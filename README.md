# Hi, I'm Sophia! 👋

I'm a cancer genomics researcher and computational biologist (Ph.D.) based in Freiburg, Germany 🇩🇪, working at the intersection of functional genomics, proteogenomics, and translational biology.

My research focuses on alternative translation initiation, cancer gene regulation, and drug resistance mechanisms — with a particular interest in how non-canonical protein isoforms contribute to tumor biology and therapeutic escape.

I build reproducible pipelines and analysis tools in R (primary) and Python, working with large-scale genomic, proteomic, and clinical datasets to take problems from raw data to interpretable, decision-relevant results.

#### Recent work:
- [Competitive landscape analysis](https://github.com/sdegeorgia/adc-clinical-trial-landscape/) of ADC clinical trials using the ClinicalTrials.gov API — sponsor analysis, indication trends, CAGR modeling 
- Developed internal tools for [CNV detection](https://www.github.com/sdegeorgia/CANVAS-summary), [long-read viral integration](https://github.com/sdegeorgia/LAAVA-summary), and [melanoma drug resistance and phenotype switching drivers](https://github.com/sdegeorgia/Melanoma_Drug_Resistance_Data)
- Presented research at international conferences, supported by NSF and NIH fellowships
